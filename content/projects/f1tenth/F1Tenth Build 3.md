While we [[F1Tenth Build 1|build F1Tenth]] Car with 2D-Hokuyo LiDAR(10LX), we already had this LiDAR from professor. We were lucky, because the Hokuyo is by far the most expensive component from the [F1Tenth Build - Bill of Materials](https://roboracer.ai/build). I was also helping another team in our student society, and the high cost of sensors was a burden for them. At that moment we wondered 'is there any alternative to this sensor?' Then we found [RoboRacer-3DLiDAR](https://github.com/TUM-AVS/RoboRacer-3DLiDAR) from TUM. Looked promising.

So we updated our car "MiRu Car1" with 3DLiDAR(Livox MID360) and gave Hokuyo LiDAR to "MiRu Car2"
