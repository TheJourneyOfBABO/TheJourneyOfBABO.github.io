[F1Tenth (RoboRacer)](https://roboracer.ai/) is a 1/10-scale autonomous racing platform. With [[MIRU]], we built our own cars from scratch and grew the fleet to three vehicles.

![[f1car.jpg]]
*First version of the MiRu Car*

> [!success] Results
> - 1st place overall — Global Innovation Festa 2025
> - 2nd place in qualifying — ICCAS 2025
