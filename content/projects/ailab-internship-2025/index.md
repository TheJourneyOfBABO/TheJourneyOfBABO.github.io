AILAB (Automotive Intelligence Lab) at Hanyang University, Seoul, ran an internship program in summer 2025: 8 weeks from 30.06 (Mon) to 22.08 (Fri). The first 3 weeks were for study, followed by a project on the [[projects/f1tenth/index|F1Tenth]] platform.

## Weekly Notes
- **[[AILAB Internship Week1|Week 1]]** (June 30 – July 4) — C++ fundamentals and algorithm study, deep learning basics with PyTorch, and an MNIST image classification project
- **[[AILAB Internship Week2|Week 2]]** (July 7 – July 11) — Sorting, BFS/DFS, and greedy algorithms; C++ inheritance and STL; CNNs and ResNets
- **[[AILAB Internship Week3|Week 3]]** (July 14 – July 18) — Trees; C++ smart pointers and threads; deep RNNs and a full training pipeline
